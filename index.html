Why should you visit Da Lat?

Da Lat has always been one of the most favorite destinations for tourists thanks to its cool climate and beautiful scenery.

Reasons to visit this city:

  • Year-round cool and fresh climate.
  • Poetic natural landscapes with many pine hills.
  • Diverse and delicious street food.
